Animal Shelter Management

An animal rescue and rehabilitation center has approached your company to implement a shelter management solution using Odoo. Their requirements are as follows:

Animals

The rescued, rehabilitated, and resident animals to be managed within the shelter module. Animals include the following attributes based on the current system schema:

  • Nickname: A descriptive name for the individual animal (Required).
  • Picture: An image upload field for the animal's profile (Max dimensions: 500x500 pixels).
  • Birth Date: The estimated or known date of birth for the animal.
  • Description: A detailed text summary of the animal's history, behavior, or special needs.
  • Gender: The sex of the animal (Male or Female, Required).
  • Weight: The current weight of the animal.
  • Arrival Time: The precise date and time the animal was admitted to the center.
  • Status: The current lifecycle stage of the animal (Not Received, At The Center, or Released). Defaults to "Not Received".
  • Is Vaccinated: A checkbox indicating vaccination status. Defaults to True.
  • Diet: The dietary classification of the animal (Herbivore, Carnivore, or Omnivore).
  • Animal Type: A categorization linking the animal to a specific species group (e.g., Canine, Feline, Raptor).
  • Feeding Time: The scheduled daily time for feeding the animal.
  • Vaccines: A list of medical vaccines administered to the animal.
  • Permitted Release Countries: A list of target geographical regions where the animal is legally allowed to be re-introduced or adopted.
  • Location: The specific pen, enclosure, or wing within the facility where the animal is housed.
  • Sponsor: The external partner (individual or corporate donor) sponsoring the animal’s care.
  • Assigned Vet: The internal staff veterinarian responsible for the animal’s health.
  • Vet Phone: A read-only field automatically pulling the assigned veterinarian's work phone number.
  • Final Released Country: The specific country where the animal was successfully released or adopted.

Animal Types

The species of an animal

  • Name: The species category or type name (Required).

Animal Vaccines

Medical records of preventative treatments available.

  • Name: The name of the specific vaccine (Required).

Animal Locations

The specific pen, enclosure, or wing within the facility where the animal is housed.

  • Name: The name of the specific location (Required).

Implementation Notes

  • Custom Models: Create appropriate models, views, and access rights for Animals, Animal Types, Animal Locations and Shelters. Ensure proper relational fields (Many2one, Many2many) are established between them.
  • Inheritance: Sponsors are res.partner records. Vets are existing shelter employees linked to hr.employee with a related field to fetch their work phone automatically.
  • Logic & Constraints: Implement a Python constraint (@api.constrains) ensuring neither the Permitted Release Countries nor the Final Released Country can include "Belgium". Add header buttons on the form view to trigger manual state changes to At The Center and Released.
  • Navigation: Create a new top-level menu item titled Shelter Management with appropriate sub-menus for managing animals, species types, and facility locations.
